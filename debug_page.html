What is this?

Since it's currently impossible to calculate non-event K/D in Stalzone, this is a StalzoneHQ-specific calculation aiming to estimate how a character performs in open world PvP.

How is this calculated?

((Kills + Assists * 0.5) / (Deaths * 0.33)) * 1000

Kills and Assists are simple, but why Deaths are divided by 3? Well, Stalzone doesn't seem to store amount of open world PvP deaths.

To estimate open world PvP deaths, we sum deaths from bullets, melee, explosions and bleeding only. Deaths from anomalies, as well as kills and deaths in session modes are ignored. However this still might include PvE deaths - for this reason we go easy on you and divide the deaths by 3 to raise your score!

Unfortunately the game doesn't offer a distinction for kills and deaths in events like Anomalous Blizzard - we hope this can be improved in future!

Got improvement suggestions? Let me know on StalzoneHQ Discord.
